# The Mile Makers — landing page concept

A design + copy concept for **Bas (The Mile Makers)**, a growth/positioning consultant
for the event industry. Built as feedback on his own site (themilemakers.nl/homepage2026),
which buried its proposition.

**Live:** <https://wytze-narrative.github.io/narrative-tools/themilemakers/landing-concept/>

## What it demonstrates

The core fix: Bas's own page never answers *what you actually buy, from whom, for roughly
what*. This concept leads with that.

- **Hero** states the buyer's pain + the outcome in two lines (clean, no background motion)
- **"Dit kun je bij mij boeken"** — three named, scoped offers with price anchors (the biggest fix)
- **MILE wordplay made explicit** (Merk · Indruk · Loyaliteit · Events → "mijlen")
- **"Over Bas"** answers solo-vs-agency directly, with his photo
- **Honest proof placeholders** (no testimonials/logos on his site yet)
- **One funnel:** Scan → radar → call, named consistently
- Animated growth-radar in the scan section; scroll-reveals; seamless MILE marquee

## ⚠️ It's a concept

Prices, stats, and the client quote are **placeholders** — flagged on-page (top + footer
banner). Bas's photo (`milemakers-bas.jpg`) is the temporary headshot from his own site;
he plans a new shoot. Bundled locally so it doesn't break when he updates his site.
